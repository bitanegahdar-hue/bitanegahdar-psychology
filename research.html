Research Interests

My developing research interests focus on human behavior, identity, psychological regulation, and the ways individuals respond to change within social and environmental contexts.

Human Behavior & Identity

Exploring the relationship between identity, self-perception, social roles, and the environments in which individuals construct their sense of self.

Psychological Regulation

An interest in how people regulate themselves when facing change, pressure, uncertainty, and experiences that challenge their existing sense of continuity.

Body, Environment & Self

Examining the psychological relationship between the body, objects, environment, and the construction of personal identity.

Visual Culture & Psychology

An interdisciplinary interest in the ways visual representations can contribute to understanding psychological experiences, identity, and human behavior.
